The **Inbox** page lists messages people sent you in Gmail and Slack that look like they need a reply, newest first. Each shows who sent it, where it came from and a short summary. The tab's badge counts them.

- Click a message to open it in Gmail or Slack.
- Click its **×** to dismiss it once you've dealt with it. A dismissed message doesn't come back.

To check for new messages, run [`assist inbox scan`](../cli-inbox/).
